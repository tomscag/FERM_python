Thesis
FERM passes a controlled relational sanity check; on real data the central bottleneck is not only performance, but how empirical features are normalized, combined, and charged as model complexity.

Audience
PhD supervisor / PI meeting.

One-line arc
Start from a controlled toy model to validate the mechanism, move to empirical results, then frame normalization and description length as the next methodological decisions.

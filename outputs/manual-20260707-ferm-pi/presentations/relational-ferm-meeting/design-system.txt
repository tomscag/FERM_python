Slide size
1280 x 720 px.

Palette
Warm paper background #F7F3EC, dark ink #102132, muted grey #5B6770.
Accent colors: blue #2B7BB9, teal #2FA79B, gold #D9A441, red #C84646.

Typography
Aptos Display for claims/titles.
Aptos for body and labels.
Aptos Mono only for formula blocks.

Layout grammar
Editorial research deck. Each slide has:
- thin dark top rule;
- small kicker;
- claim-style title;
- one dominant proof object or decision grid;
- concise callouts;
- quiet footer and page marker.

Chart grammar
Use generated analysis figures as embedded proof objects. Surround them with plain white panels and thin rules.

Container grammar
Rectangular callouts with 7px color stripe. No rounded cards.

Source policy
All images are project-generated outputs. No external logos or identity assets.

Known constraints
Charts are embedded as images. Text/callouts are editable in PowerPoint.
